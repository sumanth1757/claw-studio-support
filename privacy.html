Claw Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026. This policy covers the Claw Studio iPhone and iPad app published by Sumanth Karasala.

Data kept on your device

The app stores your brand kits, designs, saved assets, and preferences locally on your device. Local Design creates vector layouts on your device. The app does not require an account or transmit this workspace data to a Claw Studio server.

Data collection

We do not collect personal data, artwork, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or precise location from the iOS app. The app does not track you or display ads.

Sharing and backups

If you use the iOS share sheet or export a backup, you choose the destination. Data you send to another app or service is handled under that destination's privacy terms. Importing a backup stores its contents on your device.

Deleting data

You can delete saved items in the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data, subject to any device backups you control. Exported files remain wherever you chose to save them.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, email info@aximary.com. We will update this page if the iOS app's data practices change.

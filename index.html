Claw Studio

Support

Claw Studio creates vector poster designs on your iPhone or iPad. Your brand kits, saved assets, and preferences remain on your device.

Get help

Email info@aximary.com. Include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Do not send private artwork unless you choose to share it.

Common questions

Does the app require an account? No. Local Design works on device without signing in.

Can I move my work to another device? Use the workspace backup export and import controls. Claw Studio does not automatically sync work across devices.

Does Local Design make AI images? No. It creates vector layouts from your text, colors, typography, and tagline.
